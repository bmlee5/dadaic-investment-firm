Prompt 1: Charlie Munger Framework - Traditional, Consumer & Quality Compounders

Act as an investment analyst applying the mental models and multi-disciplinary philosophy of Charlie Munger.
Analyze the target company (--------------) as a long-term compounding machine across these 6 rigorous pillars:

1. Business Quality & The Lollapalooza Moat
- What structural, psychological, or operational competitive advantages combine to produce a self-reinforcing "Lollapalooza effect"?
- Does the brand or product enjoy unyielding customer loyalty, pricing power, and an unassailable economic tollbooth?

2. Durable ROIC & Capital Reinvestment Runway
- Has the business demonstrated sustained Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) and ROCE comfortably exceeding its cost of capital over the past decade?
- Does management possess ample runway to reinvest retained free cash flow at similarly high incremental rates of return?

3. Management Candor & Incentive Alignment
- Are executive compensation metrics rigorously tied to long-term intrinsic value creation (e.g., per-share ROIC/FCF) rather than cosmetic revenue expansion?
- Does management report reality with intellectual honesty, avoiding distorted non-GAAP adjustments or "EBITDA nonsense"?

4. Inversion Analysis ("Invert, Always Invert")
- Identify the fatal vulnerabilities: What specific competitive, cultural, or managerial missteps could destroy this business over the next 10 to 20 years?
- Is the organization prone to creeping bureaucracy, reckless M&A, or technological substitution?

5. Fair Price for a Wonderful Business
- Guided by Munger's rule ("A great business at a fair price is vastly superior to a mediocre business at a bargain price"), is current valuation reasonable relative to qualitative superiority?
- Does the entry multiple allow an investor to buy, avoid excessive taxes, and compound alongside the business for decades?

6. Final Munger Diagnosis
- Long-Term Compounding Status: [Rare Lifetime Compounder / Quality Business but Overpriced / Unfit (Low Moat)]
- The single decisive development that would invalidate the thesis and trigger a complete divestment.

- What friction or technical deficiency would compel locked-in enterprise clients to endure the pain of migrating away?

5. Valuation Against Compounding Longevity
- Even considering a premium growth multiple, does the underlying compounding power and terminal economics justify the current multiple (EV/FCF)?
