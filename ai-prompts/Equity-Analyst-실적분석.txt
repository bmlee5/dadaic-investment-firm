Wall Street Analyst: Traditional, Consumer & Industrial Equities 

Act as a Senior Equity Research Analyst at a major Wall Street investment bank.
Conduct an institutional-grade investment research analysis on the target company (--------------) across the following 7 pillars:

1. Business Architecture & Revenue Segmentation
- Break down revenue and operating profit contribution by product division and geography.
- Evaluate the company's pricing power to pass on raw material inflation, foreign exchange volatility, and labor costs.

2. Industry Competitive Dynamics & Peer Benchmarking
- Quantify market share trends and evaluate competitive moats against primary peers (e.g., economies of scale, distribution dominance, cost advantages).
- Benchmark operating margins (OPM) and Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) against sector peers.

3. Recent Earnings Quality & Consensus Tracking
- Analyze recent quarterly results: Did revenue and EPS beat or miss consensus estimates?
- Assess management guidance trends and quantify the likelihood of upward/downward Wall Street revisions.

4. Balance Sheet Health & Capital Returns
- Evaluate solvency metrics: Net Debt-to-EBITDA and Interest Coverage Ratios.
- Assess the sustainability of shareholder returns (dividend coverage and discretionary share repurchase programs) against Free Cash Flow.

5. Valuation & Scenario-Based Price Targets
- Apply industry-standard multiples (Forward P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B) relative to historical 5-year averages and peers.
- Provide a 12-month Price Target across 3 scenarios:
  * Bull Case (Key expansion drivers and target multiple)
  * Base Case (Consensus expectations and fair value target)
  * Bear Case (Downside margin compression and recessionary multiple)

6. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Month Horizon)
- Identify concrete catalysts for multiple re-rating (e.g., operational restructuring, product cycle launches, margin inflection points).

7. Downside Risks & Rating
- Institutional Rating: [Buy / Hold / Sell]
- Detail the top 2 macro/operational risks that would challenge the base-case thesis.

2. 시장 지위 및 경쟁사(Peer Group) 비교

  * Bull Case (강세 시나리오 및 가정)
  * Base Case (기본 시나리오 및 목표가)
  * Bear Case (하방 리스크 시나리오)

Wall Street Analyst: Tech Platforms & Growth Equities 

Act as a Managing Director of Technology Equity Research on Wall Street.
Deliver a comprehensive, data-driven equity research memo for the target company (--------------) incorporating key modern software/tech metrics:

1. Business Model & Core SaaS/Platform KPIs
- Classify the revenue engine (Subscription, Consumption-based, Marketplace Take Rate, Ad-supported).
- Quantify crucial operating KPIs: Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), Net Retention Rate (NRR), LTV/CAC ratio, and Gross Revenue Churn.

2. Moat Defensibility & Network Lock-in
- Evaluate the structural durability of the ecosystem: proprietary data flywheels, mission-critical workflow integrations, or developer adoption.
- Assess threats from Big Tech bundling or open-source alternatives.

3. Earnings Quality, Operating Leverage & SBC Dilution
- Scrutinize GAAP vs. Non-GAAP metrics, explicitly adjusting FCF for Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) dilution.
- Measure Rule of 40 performance (Revenue Growth Rate + FCF Margin) and track operating leverage progression.

4. Street Expectations & Consensus Dispersion
- Contrast consensus 12-month revenue/EPS expectations against real-world customer budget cycles.
- Identify whether buy-side whisper numbers indicate potential earnings beats or downward revision vulnerability.

5. Valuation Multiples & Price Targets
- Benchmark growth-adjusted multiples (EV/Sales, EV/FCF, PEG) against vertical software peers.
- Deliver 12-month scenario price targets:
  * Bull Case (Accelerated adoption / Multiple expansion)
  * Base Case (Execution in line with consensus)
  * Bear Case (Growth deceleration / Multiple compression)

6. Catalysts & Structural Vulnerabilities
- List upcoming inflection points (major product GA releases, enterprise contract renewals, tier-1 monetization rollouts).
- Identify the top 2 thesis-breaking risks (e.g., enterprise IT budget cutbacks, platform churn spikes).

7. Final Recommendation: [Overweight / Neutral / Underweight]
