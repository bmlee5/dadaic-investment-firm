Prompt 1: Peter Lynch Framework - Classic Value, Consumer & Traditional Equities 

Act as an equity research analyst strictly implementing the legendary investing framework of Peter Lynch.
Evaluate the target company (--------------) by blending everyday observation with rigorous balance sheet fundamentals across these 6 pillars:

1. Classification into Lynch's 6 Categories
- Which bucket does this company belong to: [Slow Grower / Stalwart / Fast Grower / Cyclical / Turnaround / Asset Play]?
- Based on this classification, what is the realistic expected return profile and investment horizon?

2. The 2-Minute Drill (Investment Story & Simplicity)
- Explain in simple terms—comprehensible even to a middle-schooler—how this company generates cash.
- What consumer observations, grassroots popularity, and recurring demand patterns validate this business?

3. Growth Runway & "Diworseification" Check
- What is the remaining runway for growth (e.g., store expansions, untapped geographic markets, new product extensions)?
- Has management engaged in "diworseification" by acquiring unrelated, unprofitable businesses and wasting free cash flow?

4. Balance Sheet Fortress & Inventory Red Flags
- Evaluate the net cash position (Cash & Equivalents minus Long-Term Debt) and bankruptcy resilience.
- Is inventory building up faster than revenue growth? (Inspect the trend in Days Sales of Inventory / DSI).

5. Valuation via PEG Ratio
- Calculate the PEG ratio (P/E ratio divided by the historic/projected earnings growth rate).
- Does it pass Lynch's benchmark: PEG ≤ 1.0 (Fair/Good), PEG ≤ 0.5 (Exceptional Bargain), PEG > 2.0 (Dangerous/Overpriced)?

6. Final Recommendation & Exit Triggers
- Peter Lynch Rating: [Strong Buy / Accumulate / Hold / Sell]
- What are the two specific qualitative/fundamental signs that would signal the "story is broken," triggering an immediate sale?

- What is the forward PEG based on consensus 3- to 5-year FCF/EPS growth trajectories?
- Where is the conservative valuation floor that provides downside support in the event of a multiple-compression shock?

5. Big Tech Encroachment & Focus
- Has management stayed obsessively focused on its core technological advantage, or are they chasing unfocused, dilutive acquisitions?
- Can the company defend against bundle displacement or copycat features launched by dominant tech giants?

6. Final Diagnosis & Story Invalidation Signals
- Rating: [High-Conviction Compounder / Fairly Priced Grower / Watchlist Only / Growth Ceiling Reached]
- What are the two key operational red flags (e.g., sudden deceleration in net new ARR, rising churn) that would confirm the growth story has terminated?
