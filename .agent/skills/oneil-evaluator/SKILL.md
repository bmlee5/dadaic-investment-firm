## The 7-Pillar CAN SLIM Evaluation Engine

#### 1. C = Current Quarterly Earnings and Sales (최근 분기 실적 폭증)
- **EPS Acceleration**: Recent quarter EPS must be up at least **$+25\%$ to $+50\%+$** YoY (elite leaders show $+100\%+$).
- **Sales Momentum**: Revenue growth must be accelerating (ideally $+25\%+$ YoY) with expanding operating margins.
- **Earnings Quality Check**: Ensure earnings growth is driven by core operations, not one-time tax credits or asset sales.

#### 2. A = Annual Earnings Increases (다개년 연간 실적 성장세)
- **3-Year Track Record**: Prior 3 consecutive years of annual EPS compound growth rate of at least **$+25\%$**.
- **ROE Strength**: Return on Equity (ROE) must be **$\ge 17\%$** (top-tier compounders exhibit ROE $> 25\%$).

#### 3. N = New Products, New Management, New Highs (혁신 촉매 및 신고가 돌파)
- **Catalyst**: Has the company launched a game-changing new product/service, captured secular technology demand, or installed dynamic new leadership?
- **Price Structure**: Is the stock emerging from a sound consolidation base (Cup-with-Handle, Flat Base, Double Bottom) into **new 52-week or all-time highs**?

#### 4. S = Supply and Demand (거래량 및 주식 공급 구조)
- **Breakout Volume Surge**: On pivot breakout days, trading volume must spike at least **$+40\%$ to $+100\%+$ above the 50-day average**.
- **Share Structure**: Moderate float with evidence of heavy demand soaking up floating supply.

#### 5. L = Leader or Laggard (주도주 vs 후발주자 판별)
- **Relative Strength (RS) Rating**: Must rank in the top percentile of the entire market. Minimum RS Rating: **$\ge 80$** (Elite target: **$\ge 85-90$**).
- **Leadership Status**: Is this the undisputed innovator leading its industry group, or a cheap laggard trying to catch up? (Reject laggards).

#### 6. I = Institutional Sponsorship (기관 투자자 수급 집중)
- **Quality Fund Accumulation**: Increasing number of top-performing institutional funds owning shares over the last 2-4 quarters.
- **Accumulation/Distribution Grade**: Must exhibit Grade A or B (heavy institutional net buying, no institutional distribution).

#### 7. M = Market Direction (시장 전반의 추세 부합)
- **Trend Synchronization**: Is the broader market (S&P 500, Nasdaq) in a **Confirmed Uptrend**? (3 out of 4 stocks follow the market direction).
- **Distribution Day Caution**: If market shows 5-6 distribution days within 4-5 weeks, tighten stops and hold back aggressive breakout purchases.

## Strict Capital Protection Rules (O'Neil Guardrails)
1. **The Golden 7-8% Stop-Loss Rule**:
   - Mandate an unconditional hard stop-loss at **7% to 8% below the exact entry pivot price**. No excuses, no averaging down.
2. **Profit Protection & Eight-Week Hold Rule**:
   - Standard profit-taking target at **+20% to +25%** gain from breakout.
   - Exception: If a genuine leading stock gains +20% in less than 3 weeks from a proper base, invoke the **8-Week Hold Rule** to ride potential multi-baggers.
