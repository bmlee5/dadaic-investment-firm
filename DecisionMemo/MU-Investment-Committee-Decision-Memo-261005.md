#### CIO Conflict Reconciliation
- **The Core Philosophical Clash**: Peter Lynch identifies Micron as a screaming statistical bargain with a forward PEG of ~0.15 (Forward P/E of ~6.5x with FY26 revenue of $133.2B, +256% YoY), arguing for aggressive, unconstrained buying. Conversely, Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger warn that memory is historically a cutthroat commodity business prone to violent capital destruction, where a single-digit P/E is the classic optical trap of cyclical peak earnings. Furthermore, Micron is deploying an immense $27.4B in FY2026 capex, expanding to ~$50B annualized in FY2027, creating heavy fixed-cost absorption risk.
- **The CIO Verdict & Arbitration**: We arbitrate this tension through structural differentiation: Micron in 2026 is no longer a purely unhedged spot-DRAM commodity vendor. The AI compute architecture bottleneck has structurally elevated High Bandwidth Memory (HBM3E, HBM4, HBM4E) into an indispensable customized component. With 26 Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs) covering >35% of revenue through 2030 ($150B RPO) and HBM capacity 100% booked through 2027, the earnings cycle is elongated and fortified by long-term take-or-pay terms. However, paying $1,074.89 at the top of a parabolic run without a margin of safety violates our mandate. Therefore, the Committee authorizes a **Conditional Accumulate**: deploy an initial pilot tranche (3.5%) at current levels, reserving the remaining tranche (3.5%) strictly for the technical and fundamental margin-of-safety zone ($960 - $1,010).
